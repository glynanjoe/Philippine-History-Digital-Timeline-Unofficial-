Sources and Research Documentation

This page documents the historical sources used to research the Philippine timeline. Primary sources provide evidence from the period being studied, while secondary sources help explain and interpret historical events.

Primary Sources

1. Act of the Declaration of Philippine Independence

Author/issuing authority: Ambrosio Rianzares Bautista read the declaration on behalf of the revolutionary government led by Emilio Aguinaldo.

Date: June 12, 1898.

Accessible document: Read the Act of the Declaration of Philippine Independence

How it supports the research: This document records the revolutionary government's declaration of independence from Spain. It supports the timeline entry about June 12, 1898, and helps explain the Filipino struggle for national sovereignty.

2. Treaty of General Relations Between the Philippines and the United States

Issuing bodies: The governments of the Republic of the Philippines and the United States of America.

Date signed: July 4, 1946.

Accessible document: Read the treaty through the Supreme Court E-Library

How it supports the research: The treaty recognizes Philippine independence and the relinquishment of American sovereignty, subject to provisions stated in the agreement. It directly supports the timeline entry about independence in 1946.

3. Proclamation No. 3, Series of 1986

Issuing authority: President Corazon C. Aquino.

Date: March 25, 1986.

Accessible document: Read Proclamation No. 3

How it supports the research: This official proclamation describes the new government's stated mandate to restore democracy, protect basic rights, and establish a transition to a new constitution. It provides direct documentary evidence of the political changes that followed the 1986 EDSA People Power Revolution.

4. Proclamation No. 110, Series of 1963

Issuing authority: President Diosdado Macapagal.

Date: May 20, 1963.

Accessible document: Read Proclamation No. 110 through the Supreme Court E-Library

How it supports the research: This proclamation declared June 12, 1963, a special public holiday commemorating the 1898 declaration. It helps explain the official recognition and continuing commemoration of June 12 as Philippine Independence Day.

Secondary Sources

5. Philippine History Source Book

Publisher: National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA).

Publication: 2021.

Accessible document: Read the Philippine History Source Book (PDF)

How it supports the research: This compilation provides historical documents and contextual material for Philippine history. It can help researchers identify original documents and compare accounts. Specific claims in the timeline should still be checked against the relevant documents and other reliable sources.

6. Documents Relating to Father José Burgos and the Cavite Mutiny of 1872

Authors: John N. Schumacher, S.J., and Nicholas P. Cushner, S.J.

Publication: Philippine Studies, July 1969.

Accessible article: Read the article through Ateneo de Manila University

How it supports the research: This scholarly study presents documents relating to the Cavite Mutiny and Father José Burgos. It helps researchers examine differing accounts of the event and its relationship to the execution of GOMBURZA and the growth of Filipino nationalism.

Research and Verification Method

  1. Identify the date, people, and major claims associated with each timeline event.
  2. Consult original documents, official proclamations, treaties, and historical records when available.
  3. Compare interpretations with scholarly publications and credible historical institutions.
  4. Check that each reference opens and actually supports the claim for which it is cited.
  5. Record the image creator, title or description, date when available, source page, and usage or license information for every image used.

A source being official does not automatically mean it independently verifies every historical claim. Primary documents are evidence of what their authors or issuing authorities recorded; researchers must consider their purpose, perspective, and limitations.
